FAQ accordion

What is Glassmorphism?
A UI trend that mimics frosted glass — translucent backgrounds, backdrop-filter: blur(), soft inset highlights and floating shadows.
Why use Tailwind for it?
Utility-first CSS lets you compose glass surfaces inline: backdrop-blur-xl bg-white/55 border border-white/45. No preprocessor, no theming layer.
Performance tips?
Cap nested blur layers (max 2-3 visible at once), prefer transform + opacity for animations, and respect prefers-reduced-motion.
