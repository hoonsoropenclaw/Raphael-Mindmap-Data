What this page is

This page is a deliberate test fixture for cross-browser visual regression. The toggle above flips the page between v1 (baseline) and v2 (candidate). The test harness captures screenshots in both states, computes pixel-level diffs across Chromium, Firefox and WebKit on multiple viewports, and renders an HTML report.

Why pixelmatch

Per-pixel diffing is the most direct way to expose rendering differences across engines. We use a 0.10 threshold and a 0.5% mismatch budget per browser×viewport cell. Cells over budget are marked REGRESSED; under, PASS.
